# Folu Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Folu is a personal PDF and EPUB reader. The app is designed to keep reading data on the user's Apple devices.

## Data stored on your devices

Imported book files, reading positions, bookmarks, and local preferences are stored locally on the device. Folu does not sell or use this data for advertising.

## Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Folu stores reading positions, bookmarks, library metadata, and file identity information in the app's private iCloud container so they can be shared across the user's iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Apple controls access to that container through the user's Apple account and iCloud settings.

Folu does not upload book contents to a Folu-operated server.

## Diagnostics and support

Folu does not require an account. If you contact support through GitHub, information you include in that issue is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Do not include book contents or other sensitive information in an issue.

## Contact

Support is available through the [Folu GitHub repository](https://github.com/JunHyeokSeo/folu-reader/issues).
