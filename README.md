# Folu

Folu is a focused PDF and EPUB reader for your own library. It works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with optional iCloud sync for reading positions and bookmarks.

## Support

### Getting started

1. Add a PDF or EPUB from the library screen.
2. Open the book to continue reading.
3. In page mode, tap or click the left and right sides to move between pages. On Mac, the left and right arrow keys also move between pages.
4. Tap or click the reader to show the controls. Drag the progress bar to jump to another page.

### iCloud sync

 iCloud sync is optional and is requested once during the first launch. When enabled, Folu stores reading positions, bookmarks, and library metadata in the user's private iCloud container. The book files remain on each device unless the user imports them there.

If sync is delayed, keep the device online and choose **Sync now** in Settings. Sync also runs when the app becomes active and periodically while it is open.

### Contact

For help or to report a problem, [open an issue](https://github.com/JunHyeokSeo/folu-reader/issues) with the device, OS version, file format, and steps to reproduce the problem. Please do not upload copyrighted books or other private files.
